## Why this license

PolyForm Noncommercial best matches the requested priority: public, modifiable source without granting a general right to build a commercial business from this demo. Unlike Creative Commons NC, it is designed for software.

AGPL-3.0 would be the better option for genuine open source with network copyleft: it allows commercial businesses but requires qualifying modified network-served versions to offer corresponding source. It does not ban competitors. Bitwarden uses GPL/AGPL for its open-source core and separate source-available terms for selected commercial modules; simply using AGPL would not accomplish a noncommercial restriction.

PolyForm Shield or Perimeter can permit some commercial use while restricting competing products, but that is a different scope than a noncommercial demo. A custom license introduces interpretation and maintenance burdens. No custom legal terms have been invented here.

This is a practical licensing summary, not legal advice. Have counsel review ownership, contributor rights, upstream obligations, and a commercial license before selling or releasing the full application.
