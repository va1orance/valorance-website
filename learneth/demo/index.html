03 / Your model

Choose your AI

Use an OpenAI-compatible HTTPS API. Requests go directly from your browser to the endpoint below; it must support CORS.

Optional images use the same key and base URL, only on explicit request. Requires /images/generations with base64 PNG, JPEG or WebP output. Many providers, including OpenRouter routes, may not support this endpoint. Leave blank to disable.

Use a disposable, low-limit key. Revoke it with your provider when finished. No fallback models or automatic retries.

What leaves this page, and what stays?

Your key is sent only as an Authorization header to the chosen endpoint. Material and conversation are sent each turn. This website writes no session state to browser storage, cookies, databases or URLs. Reloading, leaving, or End & clear clears memory state. Extensions, password managers and developer tools may access inputs; secure memory erasure is not guaranteed.

The endpoint and downstream model providers may retain your requests. OpenRouter requests additionally deny data collection and fallback providers. Other endpoints receive no such retention guarantee.

Each turn sends the session history to your provider.

Up to 20 requests per session; 1,200 output tokens per chat request. Limits are not a spending guarantee. No automatic retries.
